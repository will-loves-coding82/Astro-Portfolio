<p class="primary-body"> 
Drawing has been a hobby of mine for the past five years, and lately I've been finding my way back to it. I tend to
jump between ideas quickly — I get excited about new concepts and lose interest in old ones just as fast. For a long
time, that restlessness was compounded by Adobe Illustrator's price tag, which made it hard to justify keeping the
hobby alive. That changed when I discovered Affinity.
</p> 

<br></br>

<p class="primary-body"> 
Affinity gave me an affordable way to pick up where I'd left off, and I spent time revisiting a lot of the unfinished
concepts I'd shelved over the years. The learning curve was real, but some hands-on experimentation got me up to speed
quickly. Below are a few pieces I made along the way.
</p>
